As a media consumer, I want to be able to keep an archive of the pieces of media that I consume. Whether it is books, movies, tv shows, or video games. I want to have an app that allows me to easily do this and also allows me to be able to compare to different people online. I want to be able to showcase the best of the best media I consume.

Design level 1
In this diagram the user is shown using the app to showcase their consumed media

Design level 2
This diagram goes into more detail. It shows the user actually getting media to log/showcase on their "shelf". Then downloading the app. The user then has to actually leave a review of the media and rank accordingly. Then add to their showcase.

Design level 3
The user will spend time enjoying their media of choice. After consuming the user will then come to "Shelf" where they will make an account and use the media database to find the media they want to showcase. Once they find the showcase they can officially rank and then review their media. After that they can showcase it on their account. Then the user can look at other accounts and see their reviews on the same media or a completely different form of media.
